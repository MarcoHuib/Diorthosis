Concrete findings from inspecting all263 groups:

- `Voor het gesprek heeft de manager de agenda voorbereid` and `Na controle verstuurde zij het rapport gisteren` are grammatical; annotated reorder should be considered for withdrawal rather than counted as a required grammar repair.
- `The team have approved the change` can be standard British collective agreement. `This data are` needs a register/demonstrative decision, with `This data is` and `These data are` considered together.
- `Please note the deadline is Friday` does not necessarily require a colon; commas before `but`/`maar` can be optional. Punctuation references need alternatives and an obligatory-versus-style distinction.
- `De team Atlas` has an article problem outside many annotated local spelling spans. `Please seperate the two report/documentation` leaves additional count/plural problems after spelling repair.
- Greetings to planning/documentation and signed/received Team Atlas objects expose unrealistic template substitution. Adding unrelated status sentences does not create independent language diversity.
- Whole-clause spans and combined `have send`, `Wait lets`, `informations are` repairs need explicit dependency/category review. Exact-group annotation consistency is not linguistic validity.
