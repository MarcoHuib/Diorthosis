Baseline: `9801b57266fa96dd4b42efd731d33b84a177e160`, clean `feat/local-ai-benchmarks`, existing PR #2 open/unmerged, six publish/native-start targets and test jobs green. Pre-existing benchmark files are frozen in `benchmarks/phase2/v1/baseline-artifacts.json`. Three fresh AI reviewer contexts inspect evaluator, safety, and data/inference/pipeline scopes; these are technical AI reviews, not human linguistic approval.

| Work | Scope and alternatives | Risks | Objective exit criteria |

Logical checkpoints include tests and immutable evidence. Substantial B–G work goes on a coherent dependent branch/PR targeting PR #2's unmerged branch. No main changes, merges, force pushes or releases. Independent Dutch/English judgments, final held-out approval and legal release decisions remain specifically blocked until real qualified reviewers/approvals are supplied; unrelated engineering proceeds.
